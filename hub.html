The foundation course

地基課程 · the foundation, taught as a course

The foundation of the course is Part 0 and Chapters 1 to 7 of the Beginner's Guide: chemistry, biomolecules, proteins, DNA and genes, the cell, and how a cell actually works. Read on its own, the foundation is the one part of the kit that was never finished — its only glossary lived in the Exam Pack, at the far end of the book.

  • Eight modules in a fixed order, 430 minutes of reading, with a reading budget and a goal for each one.
  • Every module carries its media: the Cantonese chapter album to listen to, the short video guides that reinforce it, and the slice of the quiz that checks it.
  • The whole 905-term vocabulary is in the book — 582 terms belong to the foundation chapters, the other 323 are the forward references the lectures will use. Nothing is defined "somewhere else in the book".

Self-test quiz

自我測驗 · 905 條術語

Multiple choice on every one of the 905 terms, drawn from the same explanations that the narration reads out. Answer and you are told immediately whether you were right, with the reason — and you can play the term's own Cantonese recording right there on the spot.

  • Filter by chapter (Ch00–Ch12) or by question type: term → meaning, meaning → term, sentence fill-in, concept.
  • 中／英 兩版，頂部一按切換。Shuffled every time; wrong answers can be redone at the end.
  • The page loads its question bank with fetch(), so it needs to be served over http: run python3 -m http.server in this folder and open http://localhost:8000/_site/quiz.html, or use the published site.
