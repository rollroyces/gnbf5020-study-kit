Real-time PCR vs droplet digital PCR

_video/assets/L05_Real_time_PCR_vs_droplet_digital_PCR.mp4

即時定量 PCR 與微滴式數位 PCR

_video/assets/L10_Autoantibody_vs_anti_drug_antibody.mp4
